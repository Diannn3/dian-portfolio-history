## The idea

PASADA explores what happens when a jeepney route becomes visible as a live system instead of something passengers have to infer by waiting at the roadside.

## Passenger layer

The passenger experience centers on route visibility, expected arrival, queue context, and occupancy signals. The interface should answer a practical question quickly: *what is moving toward me, and how long am I likely to wait?*

## Operations layer

The same spatial information can be reframed for drivers, cooperatives, and local government: where demand is building, where queues are forming, and how service is distributed across a route.

## Design direction

The visual language uses moving route traces, waiting nodes, queue density, and heat fields. The goal is to make a transport system readable without turning it into a generic dashboard.

## Current status

PASADA is represented here as a prototype / concept. No deployment, ridership, government adoption, or operational outcome is claimed.
