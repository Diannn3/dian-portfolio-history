## Challenge

Passengers can have poor visibility into vehicle arrival and availability, while drivers and cooperatives have limited shared visibility into where demand is building.

## Approach

PASADA explores live vehicle visibility, passenger queues, ETA information, demand heatmaps, and dashboards for transport operations.

## System

The prototype is framed as a network of passenger-facing and operator-facing interfaces around a shared transport state. The emphasis is on spatial clarity rather than inventing precision the underlying data cannot support.
