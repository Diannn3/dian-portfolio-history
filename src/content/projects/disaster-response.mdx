## The idea

The platform explores a universal emergency-reporting gateway for situations where normal app connectivity cannot be assumed. A short human report should be enough to begin building a structured incident record.

## Agent workflow

The concept links several reasoning steps into one incident pipeline: understand the report, resolve approximate location, request missing critical information, identify possible duplicates, bring in hazard and geospatial context, estimate urgency, and keep the incident state updated as more information arrives.

## Resilience first

The interaction model is intentionally channel-agnostic. The system is designed around the possibility that a lightweight communication channel may remain available even when a richer app experience does not.

## Spatial intelligence

Location is treated as more than a pin. Nearby hazards, access constraints, vulnerable facilities or populations, and duplicate reports can change how the same text report should be interpreted.

## Current status

This is a concept. The portfolio does not present it as a deployed, validated, or government-integrated emergency system.
