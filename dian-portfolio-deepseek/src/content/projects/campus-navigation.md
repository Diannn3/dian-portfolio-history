## Context

This work explores indoor and campus navigation for university environments, including building-level maps and route-oriented interfaces.

## Problem

Conventional map tools become less useful inside multi-floor buildings, where room labels, landmarks, stairs, corridors, and entrances matter more than road geometry.

## System direction

The prototype direction focuses on clear floor geometry, destination search, route rendering, landmark-based orientation, and visual hierarchy that remains understandable on mobile devices.

## Current state

The work is presented as an active prototype rather than a deployed navigation service.
