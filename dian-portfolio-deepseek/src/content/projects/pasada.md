## Context

PASADA explores a public-transport interface for jeepney routes, beginning with a Laguna pilot concept. It connects passenger visibility with driver and operator information rather than treating each user group separately.

## Problem

Passengers often do not know when the next jeepney will arrive, while drivers and operators have limited visibility into where demand is accumulating.

## System direction

The prototype direction includes live vehicle positions, passenger queues, ETA and occupancy information, driver demand heatmaps, and analytics views for cooperatives or local government.

## Current state

PASADA is presented here as a prototype and product concept. The portfolio does not claim live deployment or external validation unless supporting evidence is added later.
