## Context

UPPETITE Elbi is being built as a community food map for the UPLB / Los Baños area. The product centers local places, structured place information, and community contributions rather than generic restaurant discovery.

## Problem

Food around Elbi is abundant, but discovery is fragmented across word of mouth, social posts, map listings, and group chats. The project asks how a student-focused map can make that information easier to browse and maintain.

## System direction

The current direction combines a lightweight map experience, structured place profiles, contributor forms, and a verification workflow that lets non-technical teammates maintain data without editing source code directly.

## Current state

The project is actively being developed. This case study intentionally avoids claiming adoption metrics or outcomes that have not been verified yet.
