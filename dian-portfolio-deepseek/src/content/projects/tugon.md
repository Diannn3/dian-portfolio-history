## Context

TUGON is an AI-assisted disaster reporting concept for the Philippines. It is designed around degraded connectivity, multiple communication channels, geospatial context, and human review.

## Problem

During disasters, reports can be incomplete, duplicated, delayed, and distributed across channels. Response teams need structured incident information without forcing people into one perfect app or connection type.

## System direction

The proposed workflow can parse reports, resolve approximate location, request missing critical information, analyze attached media, check duplicates, query hazard and access information, estimate urgency, and update an incident through its lifecycle.
